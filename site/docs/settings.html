Docs / Reference

Settings

Open Settings with Ctrl , in the dashboard, the gear at the bottom of the sidebar, or Settings… in the tray menu. Changes save automatically.

General

SettingWhat it doesDefault
Start with Windows / Start at loginLaunches ClipTrail quietly into the tray when you sign in. Also in the tray menu.Off
Clipboard capturePause saving for 15 minutes, 1 hour, or until you resume. Handy before copying something private. Also in the tray menu and at the bottom of the sidebar.Capturing

Devices

SettingWhat it doesDefault
Sync with my devicesRuns the encrypted sync hub on your local network so paired devices share history. Off means nothing listens on the network.On
Pair a phoneShows a single-use QR code (10 minutes) and a link you can copy for pairing another computer.—
Paired devicesEach device with its online state, last seen and paired time, and a Revoke button.—
Network → PortTCP port of the hub, 1024–65535.47821

See Sync your devices.

Shortcuts

SettingWhat it doesDefault
Quick pickerGlobal hotkey for the picker. Click it and press a new combination; Esc cancels.Ctrl Shift Space
Open dashboardGlobal hotkey that shows or hides the dashboard. Can be switched off.Ctrl Alt H

Both hotkeys must be different, and need a modifier unless they're a function key. If another app already uses the combination, you'll see "… is already in use by another app".

Appearance

SettingWhat it doesDefault
ThemeSystem, Light or Dark. Applies to the dashboard and the picker.System

Privacy

SettingWhat it doesDefault
Clean links automaticallyRemoves tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid, …) from links as you copy them, before they're stored. Full list.Off
Sensitive clipsHow long one-time codes, API keys and card numbers are kept before they're deleted: 5 minutes, 15 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day or 7 days.1 hour
Excluded appsCopies made in these apps are never saved. Add an app by its executable name (e.g. keepass.exe on Windows, the process name on Linux); * works as a wildcard. Restore defaults brings back any default you removed.Common password managers

The default exclusions cover 1Password, Bitwarden, KeePass, KeePassXC, KeePassX, Dashlane, LastPass, Enpass, NordPass, Proton Pass, Keeper, RoboForm, Password Safe, Authy, GNOME Seahorse and KWallet. On Windows, ClipTrail also skips anything an app marks as "don't record" on the clipboard, which many password managers do.

Storage

SettingWhat it doesDefault
Keep historyForever, 1 year, 90 days or 30 days. Older clips are deleted automatically; pinned clips are always kept.Forever
Data folderWhere your history lives, with an Open folder button. See Data, backup & uninstall.Per OS

Where settings are stored

Desktop settings are saved as settings.json in the data folder. Settings are per device and aren't synced.
