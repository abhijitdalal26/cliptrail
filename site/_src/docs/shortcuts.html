Changing the hotkeys

  1. Open Settings (Ctrl , in the dashboard, or Settings… in the tray menu).
  2. Under Shortcuts, click the current hotkey and press the new combination. Esc cancels.

A global hotkey needs Ctrl, Alt, Shift or Win unless it's a function key (F1–F24), and Shift plus a letter alone isn't allowed because it would block typing. If another app already owns the combination, {{B}} tells you; see hotkey conflicts.
